Interning at NASA

I’m currently an OSTEM intern at NASA’s Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, Alabama.

My work sits where materials science meets machine learning. Scientists study how alloys form their internal structure as they solidify, and they record these experiments as videos with thousands of frames each. Picking out the tiny structures in every frame by hand isn’t practical, so I’m building a deep learning model that finds them automatically. The goal is to turn hours of video into reliable measurements that researchers can use to understand how these materials grow.

It’s been a great chance to apply computer vision to a real scientific problem, and to see firsthand how research gets done at NASA.
